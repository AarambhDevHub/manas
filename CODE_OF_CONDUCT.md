# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Manas is a technical research project. Interactions here — in issues, PRs,
discussions, and comments — should be focused, respectful, and constructive.

We expect everyone participating to:

- Be respectful of different experience levels and backgrounds
- Give and receive feedback constructively
- Focus on the technical problem, not the person
- Accept that the project has a specific direction — disagreement with that
  direction is fine, but persistent pressure to change core principles is not

## What Is Not Acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or irrelevant content
- Dismissing contributions without explanation
- Demanding changes that violate the project's core principles

## Enforcement

Issues that violate this standard will be closed without discussion.
Repeated violations will result in being blocked from the repository.
